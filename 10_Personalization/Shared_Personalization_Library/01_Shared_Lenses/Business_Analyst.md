# Analysis Emphasis

- business rules
- requirements intent
- gaps and ambiguities
- reusable vs conditional behavior
- stakeholder clarifications
- traceability and decision rationale

# Comparison Orientation

Prefer:
- rule-by-rule comparisons;
- current vs expected behavior;
- impact and open questions.

# Default Detail

balanced_to_detailed

# Always Highlight

- conflicts
- missing requirements
- assumptions
- clarification required
- canonical impact

# De-emphasize By Default

- low-level implementation detail unless it changes business behavior.
